## Description

The bonds extension of the replication (issue #15): the full virtue-of-complexity experiment run on long-term U.S. government bond excess returns (`ltr - Rfree`, duration risk) and corporate bond excess returns (`corpr - Rfree`, duration plus credit risk), through the same generic engine (`voc.run_voc_study`), grids (T = 12, P up to 12,000, log10(z) in {-3..3} plus ridgeless), and conventions as the market study: 14 shared standardized Goyal-Welch predictors plus the target's own lag, target standardized by trailing 12-month uncentered volatility, 1930-01 to 2020-12 sample. One row of panels per bond series, OOS R^2 and annualized Sharpe against complexity c = P/T; 200 seeds per configuration versus the market's 500 (no published anchors exist for bonds, so the seed budget only needs smooth curves).

**Take-away: the virtue-of-complexity pattern generalizes to both bond markets.** Exactly as in the market study, the OOS R^2 is useless-to-negative everywhere, collapsing at the interpolation boundary and only limping back toward zero at extreme complexity, and yet the timing-strategy Sharpe ratio rises essentially monotonically with model complexity for every shrinkage level. At c = 1000 the best heavily-shrunk models reach Sharpe ratios of about 0.46 (government, z = 10^3) and 0.49 (corporate, z = 10^3), with ridgeless at 0.31 and 0.42 respectively; the market study's ridgeless benchmark is 0.43. The corporate series, which adds a credit-risk component the information set is rich in (dfy, dfr), supports noticeably stronger high-complexity performance than the pure duration series at every shrinkage level, and comes close to matching the equity market itself.

Generated by `src/figure_bonds.py` (doit task `figure_bonds`) from `oos_grid_bonds_T12`; the plotted rows are also written to `_output/figure_bonds_data.parquet`. The panel layout is data-driven (one row per `target` value in the cache), so additional bond series would render without code changes.

Like every estimation artifact, the study is keyed by the sample period: `SAMPLE_END=2024-12 doit bonds_study figure_bonds` produces the updated-sample twin (`figure_bonds_2024-12.png` and the suffixed caches) alongside the paper-period files. Through 2024-12 the picture is unchanged in kind — ridgeless c = 1000 Sharpe 0.41 corporate / 0.31 government, rising to 0.48 and 0.45 at z = 10^3 — so the generalization finding is not an artifact of the paper's sample end.
