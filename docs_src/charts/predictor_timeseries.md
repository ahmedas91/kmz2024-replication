## Description

Small-multiples overview of the volatility-standardized dataset: the market excess return plus the 15 predictors of Kelly, Malamud and Zhou (2024), one panel per series in the paper's footnote-33 order, monthly from 1930-01. Panels share the time axis; each scales its own y-axis because standardization equalizes volatility units, not levels.

The chart's take-away is the persistence split. The valuation ratios and yield-type predictors (dp, dy, ep, b/m, lty, tbl, tms, dfy, ntis) drift slowly with first-order autocorrelations near one, while the return-type series (mkt excess, ltr, dfr, lag mkt) and svar move at high frequency with autocorrelations near zero. That split matters later: the variable-importance analysis finds the fast-moving predictors dominate short-window model performance.

Generated by `src/plot_predictor_timeseries.py` (doit task `summary_stats`) from `kmz_dataset_standardized`.
