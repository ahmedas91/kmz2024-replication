## Description

Replication of Figure 8 of Kelly, Malamud and Zhou (2024), the paper's crux result at training window T = 12: market-timing performance RISES with model complexity even though the out-of-sample R^2 (Figure 7 Panel A) stays negative. Four panels against complexity c = P/T on the paper's broken x-axis (linear over [0, 50], a break, then [990, 1000]): Panel A the annualized Sharpe ratio, Panel B the monthly alpha, Panel C the annualized information ratio, Panel D the alpha t-statistic. Alphas are versus a static position in the volatility-standardized market (the paper's caption convention). One line per ridge shrinkage level log10(z) in {-3, ..., 3} in the paper's legend colors, plus the ridgeless (z -> 0 minimum-norm) limit as a dashed dark line; the dashed vertical line marks the interpolation boundary c = 1.

The ridgeless c = 1000 cell is the quantitative anchor the replication is validated against (values independently reproduced by Nagel): alpha t-statistic near 2.81, information ratio near 0.296, Sharpe ratio above 0.4. Under the pipeline's pinned conventions (centered RFF standardization, the Goyal-Welch workbook market series as the target), this replication lands within about 1 percent of each anchor; `src/test_figure8.py` enforces them with a stated tolerance. The brief Sharpe dip near c = 1 at low shrinkage is the boundary variance spike compressing the ratio, exactly as in the paper.

Generated by `src/figure8.py` (doit task `figure8`) from `oos_grid_T12`; the plotted panel data, ridgeless rows included, is also written to `_output/figure8_data.parquet`.
